## Statement

**Proof Invariant (PI-1):** Every constitutional claim SHALL be classified as **normative**, **implemented**, **verified**, **reproduced**, or **research**.

## Normative text

1. No requirement, implementation, or architectural claim may lack an explicit maturity classification in CSR-1.0.
2. **Research** claims MUST be documented and MUST NOT be presented as verified guarantees.
3. **Verified** claims REQUIRE evidence, receipt, and anchored provenance.
4. **Reproduced** claims REQUIRE independent R1-0 confirmation.
5. COR-1.0 MUST cross-check CSR classifications against derived artifact state.

## Progression

```
Normative → Implemented → Verified → Reproduced
```

Research is an explicit opt-out of Version 1.0 guarantee boundaries.
