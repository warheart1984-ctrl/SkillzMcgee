## 1. Purpose

This mandate defines the responsibilities and obligations of stewards who protect the Version 1.0 constitutional baseline.

---

## 2. Steward Responsibilities

### 2.1 Protect Constitutional Semantics

Stewards must ensure that:

- invariants remain intact
- canonical categories remain intact
- proof graph semantics remain intact
- reproducibility guarantees remain intact

### 2.2 Enforce Constitutional Stability

Stewards must uphold the Stability Principle:

**Semantics are frozen; implementations may evolve.**

**See:** [constitutional-stability-principle-1.0.md](./constitutional-stability-principle-1.0.md)

### 2.3 Govern Amendments

Stewards must:

- evaluate amendment proposals
- require evidence
- assess compatibility
- ensure traceability
- prevent unnecessary constitutional change

### 2.4 Maintain Founder-Independence

Stewards must ensure the Constitution survives its creators.

---

## 3. Steward Obligations

Stewards must:

- act neutrally
- avoid founder bias
- govern with evidence
- maintain transparency
- protect the identity of the platform

---

## 4. Amendment Gatekeeping

Stewards must reject any proposed constitutional change that:

- does not improve implementability
- does not improve verification
- does not improve traceability
- does not improve reproducibility
- does not improve governance

Such changes belong in the operational layer.

**See:** [amendment-gatekeeping-rules-1.0.md](./amendment-gatekeeping-rules-1.0.md)

---

## 5. Status

This mandate is **normative** and **frozen** as part of Version 1.0.
