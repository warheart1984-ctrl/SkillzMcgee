## Declaration

The stewards of Continuity OS hereby declare:

**Version 1.0 of the Continuity OS Constitution is formally frozen.**

The following components are now **immutable**:

- CAR-1.0
- Constitutional invariants
- Proof graph semantics
- Evidence and provenance model
- Constitutional loop
- Semantic grammar
- Compatibility rules
- Stability principle
- Amendment procedure
- Governance charter
- Stewardship mandate

No further semantic changes may be made without initiating a constitutional amendment.

---

## Purpose of the Freeze

The freeze establishes:

- a stable constitutional baseline
- a durable semantic identity
- a foundation for independent implementations
- a platform for future evolution
- a governance framework for amendments

Version 1.0 is now a **constitutional platform**, not a project.

---

## Effective Date

Version 1.0 is frozen as of **2026-06-26**.
