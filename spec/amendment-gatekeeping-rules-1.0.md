## 1. Purpose

These rules define the criteria and obligations governing constitutional amendments for Version 2.0 and beyond.

---

## 2. Amendment Gatekeeping Rule

A constitutional amendment must be **justified before it is proposed**.

The burden of proof is on the proposer.

---

## 3. Amendment Requirements

A valid amendment must demonstrate measurable improvement in at least one of:

- independent implementability
- verification
- traceability
- reproducibility
- constitutional governance

If it does not improve these, it is **not constitutional**.

---

## 4. Amendment Prohibitions

An amendment must **not**:

- simplify implementation at the cost of semantics
- introduce hidden state
- weaken invariants
- reduce reproducibility
- alter meaning without evidence
- collapse category boundaries

---

## 5. Amendment Process

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Justification** | Evidence of constitutional need |
| **Impact Analysis** | Semantic, operational, governance |
| **Compatibility Assessment** | Backward and forward |
| **Migration Plan** | Canonical and operational |
| **Lineage Declaration** | New constitutional version |
| **Steward Approval** | Unanimous |

---

## 6. Amendment Rarity

Constitutional amendments must be:

- rare
- deliberate
- evidence-driven
- traceable
- reversible only through further amendment

---

## 7. Status

These rules are **normative** and **frozen** as part of Version 1.0.
