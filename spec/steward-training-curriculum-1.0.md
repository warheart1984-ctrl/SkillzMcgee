## 1. Curriculum Overview

This curriculum prepares stewards to protect the constitutional baseline and govern future evolution.

It consists of four modules:

1. Constitutional Foundations
2. Semantic Governance
3. Conformance and Verification
4. Amendment Stewardship

---

## 2. Module I — Constitutional Foundations

### Topics

- CAR-1.0
- invariants
- semantic grammar
- constitutional loop
- canonical artifacts
- proof graph semantics

### Competencies

- Identify constitutional vs operational changes
- Interpret canonical artifacts
- Detect invariant violations

**References:** [constitutional-baseline-1.0.md](./constitutional-baseline-1.0.md), [semantic-grammar-1.0.md](./semantic-grammar-1.0.md)

## 6. Completion Criteria

A steward is certified when they can:

- protect semantics
- govern evolution
- enforce invariants
- validate conformance
- reject unjustified amendments
- maintain founder-independence
