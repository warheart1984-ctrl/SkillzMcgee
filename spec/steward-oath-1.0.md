## The Steward Oath

As a steward of the Continuity OS Constitution, I affirm:

### 1. To protect the constitutional baseline

I will preserve the semantics, invariants, categories, and guarantees that define Version 1.0.

### 2. To uphold the Constitutional Stability Principle

I will ensure that semantics remain frozen even as implementations evolve.

### 3. To govern with evidence

I will require traceability, reproducibility, and canonical grounding for all decisions.

### 4. To maintain founder-independence

I will ensure the Constitution survives its creators and remains interpretable by future generations.

### 5. To prevent unnecessary constitutional change

I will reject amendments that do not measurably improve implementability, verification, traceability, reproducibility, or governance.

### 6. To act neutrally and transparently

I will avoid founder bias, protect the identity of the platform, and document all decisions.

### 7. To uphold the amendment process

I will require justification before proposal, evidence before change, and consensus before adoption.

### 8. To protect the integrity of canonical artifacts

I will reject hidden state, category drift, invariant violations, and semantic corruption.

### 9. To serve the Constitution above any implementation

I will treat the constitutional layer as the source of truth, not the code that executes it.

### 10. To preserve the platform for future generations

I will ensure Continuity OS remains a constitutional platform, not a mutable project.
