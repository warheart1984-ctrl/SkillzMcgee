# Chapter 3 — Conflict & Backlash

**Engine:** Negotiant Core (⟴) v1.0.0  
**Face:** RPG (validated provisional)

## 3.1 Types of Conflict

- **Direct** — you act against a clear obstacle.
- **Indirect** — you act into uncertainty or systems.
- **Internal** — you negotiate with your own tensions.

## 3.2 Conflict Procedure

1. GM defines stakes + difficulty (DC 5–15).
2. You declare **primary tension** (Becoming, Resistance, etc.).
3. Roll `1d10 + primary tension`.
4. Compare to DC:
   - **Success** → desired effect; Core spins (`coreTick`).
   - **Failure** → partial/complication; Core spins; backlash may trigger.
5. Update RPG face: `mode`, `cycle`, `backlash`.

## 3.3 Backlash Triggers

Backlash checks occur when:

- You roll with a tension **2+ above** your lowest.
- You chain **3+ actions** on the same tension.
- The zone's backlash is already **Volatile** (3–4) or **Paradox risk** (5+).

If backlash ≥ 3 → GM may introduce a **Backlash Event**.

## 3.4 Backlash Events (examples)

- **Paradox:** two truths collide; reality flickers.
- **Fracture:** relationships, factions, or zones split.
- **Echo:** past choices reassert themselves.
- **Inversion:** your mode flips to its opposite.
- **Bleed:** your tensions affect the zone or others.

See: `docs/darz/events/paradox-storm-table.md`

## 3.5 Conflict Outcomes

- **Clean success** → low backlash, stable mode.
- **Messy success** → increased backlash, unstable mode.
- **Failure** → high backlash, dramatic consequences.

Backlash is the **drama engine**; Core is the physics.
