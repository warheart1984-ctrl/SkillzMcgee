## 1. What is Continuity OS?

A constitutional runtime that enforces correctness through invariants, canonical artifacts, and reproducible semantics.

---

## 2. Why does Continuity OS exist?

Because modern systems are too complex to trust implementation-level correctness.

Continuity OS enforces correctness at the constitutional layer.

---

## 3. What does "constitutional" mean here?

It means the system has:

- a baseline
- invariants
- canonical artifacts
- a governance process
- a conformance ecosystem
- a stable semantic identity

These define **what the system is**, not how it is implemented.

---

## 4. What is frozen in Version 1.0?

- CAR-1.0
- invariants
- semantic grammar
- proof graph semantics
- evidence and provenance model
- constitutional loop
- compatibility rules
- stability principle
- amendment procedure
- governance charter

**See:** [../../spec/version-1.0-freeze-declaration.md](../../spec/version-1.0-freeze-declaration.md)

---

## 5. What is not frozen?

Everything operational:

- implementations
- tooling
- dashboards
- visualizers
- operator workflows
- documentation
- public messaging

These can evolve freely.

---

## 6. What does "constitutional compatibility" mean?

Two independent implementations must derive the same:

- constitutional meaning
- proof relationships
- governance guarantees

from the same canonical artifacts.

---

## 7. What is drift?

A constitutional signal indicating:

- mechanical variance
- semantic evolution
- invariant violation

Drift is classified as mechanical, semantic, or constitutional.

**See:** [../../spec/drift-analysis-handbook-1.0.md](../../spec/drift-analysis-handbook-1.0.md)

---

## 8. What is the Workflow Modeling Suite?

A constitutional modeling grammar:

Observation → Finding → Recommendation → Expected Outcome → Success Metric

It is the canonical structure for workflow meaning.

---

## 9. How does Continuity OS evolve?

Through the amendment process:

- justification
- evidence
- compatibility analysis
- migration plan
- steward approval

Amendments are rare and deliberate.

**See:** [../../spec/amendment-procedure-1.0.md](../../spec/amendment-procedure-1.0.md)

---

## 10. What happens in Version 1.1?

Capability expansion without constitutional change.
