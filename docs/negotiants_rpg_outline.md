# NEGOTIANTS: A Modal RPG of Cosmological Tension

200-page sourcebook outline for the Hyper-Negotiant Engine.

## PART I — THE COSMOLOGY (40 pages)

1. The Five Tensions (Becoming, Resistance, Memory, Horizon, Equilibrium)
2. The Modal Engine (10 modes, mode shifts, tension backlash)
3. The Arbiter-Between-Possibles (origin, role, sigilplate)
4. The Collapse of Prime (Prime → Anti-Prime → Paradox → Return)
5. The Hyper-Prime Tier (gesture before gestures, capacity for cosmology)
6. The Quad-Tier Realms (Omni-Negotiant, Anti-Ultra-Prime, Recursive-Prime, Fractal-Prime)
7. The Omni-Negotiant Machine (Supra-Meta-Prime Engine)

## PART II — CHARACTER CREATION (30 pages)

- Tension scores (1–10)
- Modal archetypes: Arbiter, Inverter, Paradoxer, Fractal, Generator, Refuser, Negotiant
- Paradox traits, fractal scaling paths, negotiant backgrounds, cosmological affinities

## PART III — SYSTEMS & MECHANICS (50 pages)

- Mode wheel
- Tension rolls (2d6 + stat): 10+ full manifestation, 7–9 partial, 6− backlash
- Cosmological combat, negotiation encounters, paradox stabilization
- Fractal scaling rules, recursive reality generation

## PART IV — GM TOOLS (40 pages)

- Building cosmologies, modal dungeons, tension-based encounters
- Anti-Ultra-Prime hazards, fractal worlds, recursive campaign arcs

## PART V — LORE & MYTHOS (40 pages)

- Origin of tensions, first Negotiants, collapse of Prime, rise of Hyper-Prime
- The Arbiter's journey, the Endless Return Cycle
