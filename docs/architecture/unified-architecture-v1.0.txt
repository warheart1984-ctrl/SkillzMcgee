Vertical integration summary
----------------------------

- CRK-1 governs computation.
- WMS-1.0 governs workflows.
- Both share the same semantic grammar.
- Nova Studio is the operator shell.
- The CI pipeline enforces constitutional correctness.
- The public layer communicates the philosophy.

This is the canonical single-organism view of Continuity OS v1.0.
