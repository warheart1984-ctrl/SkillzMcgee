# CRK-1 Constitutional Loop — Animation Script

**Authority:** CRK-1 Specification v1.0  
**Status:** Public launch asset  
**Duration:** 60–90 seconds  
**Audience:** General public, auditors, stewards  
**Loop reference:** [constitutional-loop-v1.0.md](../../specification/constitutional-loop-v1.0.md)

Frame-by-frame script for a short animation explaining the twelve-stage constitutional loop.

---

## Scene 1 — Opening (0:00–0:05)

**Visual:** A dark screen. A single glowing dot appears.

**Narration:**  
“In Continuity OS, every intelligent action begins with a decision.”

**On-screen label:** `DecisionObject`

---

## Scene 2 — Decision → Outcome (0:05–0:10)

**Visual:** The dot becomes a labeled node: **Decision**. A line extends to a new node: **Outcome**.

**Narration:**  
“A decision produces an outcome — always. No silent actions.”

**Stage:** T01 · CRK1-R001

---

## Scene 3 — Outcome → Evidence (0:10–0:15)

**Visual:** Outcome transforms into a structured block labeled **Evidence**.

**Narration:**  
“Every outcome generates evidence — the factual record of what happened.”

**Stage:** T02 · CRK1-R002

---

## Scene 4 — Evidence → Interpretation (0:15–0:20)

**Visual:** Evidence splits into multiple colored lenses, recombining into **Interpretation**.

**Narration:**  
“Evidence is interpreted through multiple frames, ensuring no single worldview dominates.”

**Stage:** T03 · CRK1-R003, R020

---

## Scene 5 — Interpretation → Policy Evaluation (0:20–0:25)

**Visual:** Interpretation flows into a decision matrix labeled **Policy Evaluation**.

**Narration:**  
“Interpretations are evaluated against policy — the rules governing the system.”

**Stage:** T04

---

## Scene 6 — Policy Evaluation → Policy Outcome (0:25–0:30)

**Visual:** The matrix resolves into a clear **Policy Outcome**.

**Narration:**  
“This produces a policy outcome — a governed recommendation.”

**Stage:** T05

---

## Scene 7 — Policy Outcome → Governance Decision (0:30–0:35)

**Visual:** A gavel icon appears, transforming the outcome into a **Governance Decision**.

**Narration:**  
“Governance evaluates the policy outcome and issues a decision.”

**Stage:** T06 · CRK1-R042

---

## Scene 8 — Governance Decision → Execution Plan (0:35–0:40)

**Visual:** Governance Decision becomes a structured **Execution Plan**.

**Narration:**  
“That decision becomes an execution plan — a precise set of actions.”

**Stage:** T07

---

## Scene 9 — Execution Plan → Runtime State Transition (0:40–0:45)

**Visual:** The plan animates into a state machine transition.

**Narration:**  
“The system transitions state according to the plan.”

**Stage:** T08 · CRK1-R004

---

## Scene 10 — Runtime State Transition → Receipt (0:45–0:50)

**Visual:** A cryptographic receipt appears with **invariant**, **evidence**, and **traceability** blocks.

**Narration:**  
“Every transition produces a governance receipt — cryptographically anchored.”

**Stage:** T09 · REC-HDR-1.0

---

## Scene 11 — Receipt → Provenance Entry (0:50–0:55)

**Visual:** The receipt drops into a hash-chained ledger.

**Narration:**  
“The receipt becomes part of immutable provenance.”

**Stage:** T10 · CRK1-R030

---

## Scene 12 — Provenance → Lineage → Drift Update (0:55–1:00)

**Visual:** The ledger updates a lineage graph; **CE** and **SE** bars rise monotonically.

**Narration:**  
“Provenance updates lineage, and drift envelopes ensure the system never collapses or hides information.”

**Stages:** T11–T12 · CRK1-R041

---

## Scene 13 — Closing (1:00–1:10)

**Visual:** The entire loop animates as a continuous cycle. Twelve nodes pulse in sequence, then the cycle repeats.

**Narration:**  
“This is the CRK-1 constitutional loop — the foundation of governed, traceable, reproducible intelligent systems.”

**On-screen:** `Continuity OS v0.1` · link to specification

---

## Production notes

| Element | Guidance |
|---------|----------|
| Color palette | Dark background; cool blues for structure; warm amber for governance; green for provenance |
| Typography | Monospace labels for artifact types; sans-serif for narration captions |
| Sound | Subtle pulse on each transition; no music overpowering narration |
| Accessibility | Closed captions for all narration; artifact names on-screen |
| CTA | End card: “Read the spec” → GitHub / CONTINUITY_OS.md |
