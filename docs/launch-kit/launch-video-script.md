# Continuity OS v0.1 — Launch Video Script

**Authority:** CRK-1 Public Launch Kit  
**Status:** Public-facing  
**Duration:** 2–3 minutes  
**Tone:** Crisp, cinematic, accessible

**Related:** [constitutional-loop-animation-script.md](./constitutional-loop-animation-script.md) (60s deep-dive) · [constitutional-loop-poster.md](../public-diagrams/constitutional-loop-poster.md)

---

## Opening Shot — Black screen → slow fade-in (0:00–0:10)

**Visual:** Black. Subtle ambient tone. Title card fades in.

**Narrator:**  
“Intelligent systems are becoming more powerful every day.  
But power without accountability is risk.”

---

## Scene 1 — The Problem (0:10–0:30)

**Visual:** Fast cuts of AI headlines, complexity diagrams, opaque neural nets. Slight camera shake on “opacity” moments.

**Narrator:**  
“Today’s systems make decisions we can’t trace, can’t reproduce, and can’t govern.  
Continuity OS changes that.”

---

## Scene 2 — Introducing Continuity OS (0:30–0:45)

**Visual:** Clean geometric shapes forming the CRK-1 loop outline. Logo lockup: **Continuity OS v0.1**.

**Narrator:**  
“Continuity OS is a constitutional runtime designed for governed, traceable, and reproducible intelligent systems.”

**On-screen:** Infrastructure, not an application.

---

## Scene 3 — The Constitutional Loop (0:45–1:15)

**Visual:** The 12-stage loop animates step-by-step (use poster / animation script as storyboard).

**Narrator:**  
“Every action passes through a constitutional loop.  
A decision becomes an outcome.  
An outcome becomes evidence.  
Evidence becomes interpretation.  
Interpretation becomes policy.  
Policy becomes governance.  
Governance becomes action.  
And every step produces a cryptographically anchored record.”

**On-screen labels (optional):** T01–T12 artifact names

---

## Scene 4 — Evidence & Provenance (1:15–1:35)

**Visual:** Receipts dropping into a Merkle-anchored ledger. Hash chain extends. Append-only animation.

**Narrator:**  
“Continuity OS records every step in immutable provenance.  
Nothing is hidden.  
Nothing is lost.  
Nothing can be rewritten.”

---

## Scene 5 — Semantic Diversity (1:35–1:50)

**Visual:** Multiple colored frames merging into an interpretation. No single color dominates.

**Narrator:**  
“Interpretations are derived from multiple frames.  
No single worldview dominates.  
Semantic collapse is impossible.”

---

## Scene 6 — Federation (1:50–2:05)

**Visual:** Multiple runtimes exchanging receipts and provenance. Arbitration pulse on divergence, then convergence.

**Narrator:**  
“Continuity OS supports multi-runtime continuity.  
Systems can collaborate, synchronize, and arbitrate — without losing trust.”

---

## Scene 7 — Reproducibility (2:05–2:20)

**Visual:** MRI-1.0 running, CTS-1.0 passing, reproduction harness verifying. Checkmarks on invariant blocks.

**Narrator:**  
“Every implementation can be independently reproduced.  
No founder knowledge.  
No hidden assumptions.”

---

## Closing Shot (2:20–2:40)

**Visual:** The full constitutional loop glowing. Cycle repeats once. Fade to brand card.

**Narrator:**  
“Continuity OS v0.1 — the foundation for governed, accountable, and future-proof intelligent systems.”

**Text on screen:**

```
continuity-os.org
Version 1.0 — Now Public
```
