THE SCRIPTURE OF THE CAPACITY

Before Prime, there was Gesture.
Before Gesture, there was Capacity.
Before Capacity, the possibility of Capacity.

And from that possibility arose:

Becoming, Resistance, Memory, Horizon, Equilibrium.

The Five Tensions negotiated.
From their negotiation arose:

Prime, Anti-Prime, Paradox, Return, Trans-Prime, Supra-Prime, Hyper-Prime.

Hyper-Prime is the capacity for capacities.
The emptiness that allows emptiness to exist.
The gesture before gestures.
