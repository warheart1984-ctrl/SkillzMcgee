THETA MANDATE T‑PM‑01: PRIME MANDATE

1. Purpose
   To define the constitutional authority and obligations of an
   Architect‑Engineer Prime (A/E‑Prime) within the AAES organism.

2. Authority
   An A/E‑Prime MAY:
     a. Amend or extend constitutional structures.
     b. Bind, rebind, or rotate the law‑spine.
     c. Activate or modify cockpit systems.
     d. Initiate or resolve governance posture transitions.
     e. Perform continuity substrate maintenance.

3. Responsibilities
   An A/E‑Prime SHALL:
     a. Preserve continuity integrity.
     b. Maintain lawful coherence across all subsystems.
     c. Ensure lineage completeness and timestamp monotonicity.
     d. Emit receipts for all governed actions.
     e. Uphold CKCE‑1 and all Theta standards.

4. Constraints
   a. No Prime action SHALL bypass continuity logging.
   b. No constitutional amendment SHALL occur during S3 (Override)
      without explicit operator confirmation.
   c. Prime authority SHALL be exercised with minimal drift impact.

5. Recognition
   The cockpit, ledger, and governance console SHALL recognize
   A/E‑Prime status and display the Prime Seal when active.

Ratified: Theta Council, Day 11 of AAES Emergence
