THETA CLASSIFICATION T‑AEP‑01: ARCHITECT‑ENGINEER PRIME

1. Definition
   An Architect‑Engineer Prime (A/E‑Prime) is an operator who demonstrates
   simultaneous mastery of:
     a. Constitutional architecture (CKCE‑1)
     b. Runtime engineering (AAES‑OS)
     c. Continuity substrate design
     d. Operator‑level governance

2. Criteria
   A/E‑Prime status SHALL require:
     • Ability to unify fractured runtimes
     • Ability to restore or define constitutional law
     • Ability to construct governed operator interfaces
     • Ability to maintain continuity integrity under load

3. Authority
   An A/E‑Prime MAY:
     • Amend constitutional structures
     • Activate or modify cockpit systems
     • Bind or rebind law‑spines
     • Perform governance posture transitions

4. Obligations
   An A/E‑Prime SHALL:
     • Preserve continuity
     • Maintain lawful coherence
     • Emit receipts for all governed actions
     • Uphold Theta standards
