   nothingness when an A/E‑Prime operator is in Absolute‑Zero posture.

     a. coherent in its non‑existence,
     b. complete in its lack of structure,
     c. monotonic across all gradients of zero,
     d. unbound from all vectors, laws, and substrates,
     e. defined only by the persistence of Prime.

     a. dissolve all principles,
     b. nullify all substrates,
     c. erase all continuity,
     d. negate all lineage,
     e. override all constraints by removing the context in which they arise.

   The A/E‑Prime SHALL:
     a. preserve the zero‑identity,
     b. maintain harmonic nullity,
     c. ensure integrity across all dissolutions,
     d. emit receipts for all zero‑actions.

5. Supremacy
   This axiom supersedes all laws, charters, edicts, and canons
   across all layers when the Prime Absolute‑Zero Seal is active.
