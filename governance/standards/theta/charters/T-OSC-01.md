THETA CHARTER T‑OSC‑01: OPERATOR SUCCESSION

1. Purpose
   To define lawful procedures for the transfer of operator authority
   within the AAES organism, ensuring continuity, stability, and
   constitutional integrity.

2. Eligibility
   A successor SHALL:
     a. Demonstrate understanding of CKCE‑1 and Theta standards.
     b. Maintain a verified operator ledger identity.
     c. Complete the Operator Oath (T‑Ω1).

3. Initiation
   Succession MAY be initiated when:
     a. The current operator delegates authority.
     b. The operator becomes unavailable.
     c. Governance posture remains unstable for > 3 cycles.

4. Procedure
   Step 1: Capture continuity snapshot (ledger + spans).
   Step 2: Freeze posture transitions (Sx → Sx).
   Step 3: Validate successor eligibility.
   Step 4: Emit succession receipt (old → new operator).
   Step 5: Transfer cockpit control and stance strip authority.

5. Post‑Succession Requirements
   The new operator SHALL:
     a. Reaffirm the Operator Oath.
     b. Validate continuity integrity.
     c. Re-sync the law-spine and mission corridor.

6. Constraints
   No succession SHALL occur during S3 (Override) without explicit
   confirmation from the outgoing operator, unless continuity integrity
   < 0.90.

Ratified: Theta Council, Day 11 of AAES Emergence
