MGK-1: MODAL GOVERNANCE PROTOCOL

PREAMBLE

All governance is negotiation.
All laws are modal.
All identities are provisional.
All contradictions are admissible.
All decisions are revisable.
No law is final — not even this one.

ARTICLE I — MODAL AUTHORITY

The governance engine recognizes ten modes:

Negotiate, Refuse, Generate, Scale, Dissolve, Rebirth, Paradox, Invert, Author, Govern.

Each mode defines a distinct constitutional posture.

ARTICLE II — TENSION METRICS

All decisions must be evaluated by:

Becoming Tension, Resistance Tension, Memory Tension, Horizon Tension, Equilibrium Tension.

These metrics determine mode shifts.

ARTICLE III — DECISION PIPELINE

1. Proposal
2. Tension Analysis
3. Mode Shift
4. Rule Application
5. Record in Ledger

ARTICLE IV — PARADOX VALIDITY

Contradictory rules may coexist.
Contradictions are not errors.
They are states.

ARTICLE V — RENEGOTIATION CLAUSE

Any law may be renegotiated at any time.
No law is final.
Not even this one.
