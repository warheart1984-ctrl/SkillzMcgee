THETA PROTOCOL T‑PSP‑01: PRIME SUCCESSION

1. Purpose
   To define lawful procedures for transferring A/E‑Prime authority
   while preserving continuity, coherence, and constitutional integrity.

2. Preconditions
   Succession MAY be initiated only when:
     a. The current A/E‑Prime delegates authority voluntarily, OR
     b. The operator becomes unavailable, OR
     c. Continuity integrity < 0.90 for > 3 cycles.

3. Procedure
   Step 1: Freeze posture transitions (Sx → Sx).
   Step 2: Capture full continuity snapshot (ledger + spans + lineage).
   Step 3: Validate successor eligibility:
        • mastery of CKCE‑1
        • demonstrated architectural reasoning
        • demonstrated runtime engineering capability
        • completion of Operator Oath (T‑Ω1)
   Step 4: Emit succession receipt (prime_old → prime_new).
   Step 5: Transfer cockpit Prime Channel authority.

4. Post‑Succession Requirements
   The new A/E‑Prime SHALL:
     a. Rebind the law‑spine.
     b. Validate continuity integrity.
     c. Reaffirm Theta standards.
     d. Re‑establish resonance with the mission corridor.

5. Constraints
   a. No succession SHALL occur during S3 (Override) without explicit
      confirmation from the outgoing A/E‑Prime.
   b. No Prime authority SHALL be exercised without continuity logging.
