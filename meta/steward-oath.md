# Version 1.0 Steward Oath

**Authority:** CRK-1 Specification v1.0  
**Status:** Ceremonial and ethical binding for certified stewards  
**Related:** [steward-exam.md](../conformance/certification/steward-exam.md)

## The Steward Oath

I swear to uphold the Constitution of CRK-1.  
I will preserve the invariants, enforce the contracts, and maintain the integrity of the constitutional loop.

I will protect continuity.  
I will ensure that every action produces evidence, every outcome is traceable, and every interpretation is reproducible.

I will preserve history.  
I will maintain immutable provenance, reject rewriting, and defend the integrity of the ledger.

I will uphold semantic diversity.  
I will prevent collapse, preserve multiplicity, and ensure that no single frame dominates.

I will reject hidden state.  
I will expose structure, enforce transparency, and maintain replayability.

I will ensure founder independence.  
I will remove tribal knowledge, document all assumptions, and guarantee reproducibility.

I will act as a steward, not an owner.  
I will maintain the system for those who come after me.

**This I swear, as a Steward of Continuity.**

---

*Verification:* oath acceptance is recorded in steward certification records and verified by CTS, receipts, and provenance (R031, FIA).
